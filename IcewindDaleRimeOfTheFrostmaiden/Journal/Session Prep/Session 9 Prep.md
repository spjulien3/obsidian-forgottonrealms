# Overview
The Party is lost in the woods on the way to the Black Cabin, They are going to slowly enter the White moose quest of Lonelywood. Entirely possible they side step this quest but it is not expected that they complete this quest just want them to see the moose and establish that this is not a normal moose. Then they possibly meet up and continue to [[The Black Cabin]]

## White moose
See [[White Moose]], Should the party find the White Moose and get more lost, They may try to fight the moose or befriend it but he is resistant with a Persuasion DC of like 28. The only things the Moose says is :
- "I am no one's prey"
- "This forest is mine"
- "BEGONE"
He will then try to fight if the party is still split up or run away when more show up

# Overview
The party will partake in a concert thrown by Finlay at the Luskan Arms in [[Targos]]. After this the party will make their way towards [[Lonelywood]] or [[Termalaine]] and continue to head north. They may try to make the climb to [[Kelvin's Cairn]] to try and see if they can bring back [[Garrett Velryn]] and the rest of the adventuring party from their trip. They may also continue north towards the [[The Black Cabin]] to find [[Copper Knobbernocker]]'s friend, [[Macreadus]].

## Travel Time
To Termalaine from Targos 4 hours by default, 3 hours on snowshoes, 2 hours by sled/vehicle

To Lonelywood from Termalaine 2 hours by default, 1 hour 30 minutes on snowshoe, 1 hour by vehicle

Black Cabin from Lonelywood 3 hours by default, 2 hours 20 minutes snowshoe, 1 hour and 30 minutes by vehicle

## The Party at Luskan Arms
Finaly wants to throw a party for the owner of the Luskan Arms [[Owenn Tarsenel]] to help everyones mood in town, While he does so the town speaker [[Naerth Maxildanar]] will eventually come downstairs to see what the commotion is all about. Finaly has a chance to notice that Nareath recognizes him and tries to stay relatively out of sight from him. 

## Journeying Elsewhere
Unless the party wants to navigate outside the 10 towns, their journey on the roads are relatively safe unless it is dark out. 

## Black Cabin
See [[The Black Cabin]] section, but their journey through the lonely woods is quiet and spooky. There is no wildlife their and not even trees are creaking and making noise

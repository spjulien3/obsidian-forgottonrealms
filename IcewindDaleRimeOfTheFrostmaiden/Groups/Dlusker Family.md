A Patriar family in [[Baldur's Gate]] that supported the wrong side during and uprising during the mid 1400s. This left the family on the periphery of noble society and almost destitute. They have been leveraging the few businesses they have but have been reaching into more unsavory ventures as of late. [[Lord Norold Dlusker]] had been reaching into illegal trading of weapons enemies of the city such but was caught by Hraeldar Thunderfist and his family by accident. Norold panicked and ordered his closest guards to capture these Orcs and bring them to his home where he proceeded to hold onto them until he knew how to get rid of them.

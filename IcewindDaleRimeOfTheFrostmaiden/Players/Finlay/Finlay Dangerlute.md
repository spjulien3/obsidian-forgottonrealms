Born in a particularly reclusive elven society, Finlay Dangerlute left his home to seek adventure and a different life in the world. He worked in Bauldur's Gate trying to make a name for himself as a bard but there was a lot of struggles trying to make a name in such a big city. After playing a few shows he was approached by members of The Guild. He began doing a long string of jobs for the guild, never anything too serious just petty crimes and theft from other lower level tasks for the guild. One day he was asked to break into a Patriar family's home and recover ledgers that showed some of the crimes that The Guild could use to black mail the head of the family. He worked to do so and discovered that this patriar was trafficing people and they had several Orcs held in captivity in their basement. Finlay saved the Orcs from their fate. In return they gave him a powerful Orc Stone artifact that calls upon the Orc's ancestors to fight with them. Upon doing this and keeping up his double life of playing as a bard by day and Guild member by night he was suddenly knocked out a day after the incident having just finished another show. All he remembers is a black and yellow banner with a flying snake chasing after a ball.

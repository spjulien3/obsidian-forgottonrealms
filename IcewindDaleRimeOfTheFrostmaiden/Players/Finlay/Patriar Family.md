The family that Finlay was tasked with breaking into was the Dlusker family home, well what was left of it. The history of the family is essential that they backed the wrong side during an attempt to take over the city by an usurper and his legion of lycanthropes. They were left on the edge of being able to stay in the upper city of Baldur's gate. Since they already having a bad reputation in the city, they began dealing in illicit business ventures some of which involved the [[Zhentarim]] Black Network. This connection is where Finaly's disappearance came from. The [[Zhentarim]] found out that the Dlusker's started to traffic people and they immediately looked to cover up the incident so it did not draw the attentions of the Harpers or the Lords Alliance.

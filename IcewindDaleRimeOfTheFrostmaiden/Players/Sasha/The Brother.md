Sasha's Brother was the only survivor of that blizzard, He woke up to being covered in his mother and father's coats wrapped around him with a barely smoldering fire. He later found their bodies. He cursed the sky, wind, and snow when suddenly a blue skinned icicle covered woman descended down from the storming snow, asking him if he wanted the power to never feel like this again. if he wanted to never be brutalized by the cold. He said yes and began to serve as a druid that worships the frost and Auril doing whatever she asks of him. He also changes his name to Ravasin.

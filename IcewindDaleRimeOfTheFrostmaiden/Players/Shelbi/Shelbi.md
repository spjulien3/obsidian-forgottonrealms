Shelbi is a shield drawf ice fisher barbarian who was born here in Icewind Dale. She is stoic, loud, and lacks delicacy and restraint. 

## Past Lives
Shelbi is at her first life, an asimar that was punished to live out multiple lifetimes as punishment for her hubris during the Empire of Nethril. She was an celestial being that served the old great god of magic Mystryl. Shelbi was a key factor in providing and translating the knowledge that Ioulaum recieved from Terraseer used to create the Mythallar that kept the cities afloat and all the magical devices working. These Mythallar and the [[Netheril]] Empire's hubris caused the most catastrophic even leading to the disappearance of magic for hundreds of years on top of killing the god of magic Mystryl. Shelbi Then proceeded to continue to live out half of her celestial life only for the new god of magic Mystra to curse her to keep reincarnating till she learned from her hubris and to think about the consequences of her actions on top of learning to care about others.

# Overview
This is a cabin on the northern side of the [[Lonelywood]] forest where rangers used to frequent. Now [[Macreadus]] takes refuge in this cabin while he works on a cure for the [[Everlasting Rime]] by creating a small [[Mythallar]] that he hopes will work. This [[Summer Star]] ends up killing him and leaving him in the [[Ethereal Realm]] haunting the place where he recently died. Anyone who touches the [[Summer Star]] will instantly activate the device and anyone within 10 feet needs to make a DC 17 constitution or take 10 D10 Radiant damage. Should anyone die they will be turned to dust be transported to the [[Ethereal Realm]].
